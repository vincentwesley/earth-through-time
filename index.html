    About the data

    The continents are a real plate-tectonic reconstruction, not an animation. Every coastline and block of continental crust is rotated by its plate's motion from the Merdith et al. (2021) 1 billion-year full-plate model by the EarthByte Group, computed ahead of time with pyGPlates at 1 Myr steps.

    Darker green is continental crust; lighter green is the shape of today's land carried back in time. Old positions are uncertain, especially before about 400 Ma, and the frame is palaeomagnetic, so longitudes are less certain than latitudes. The lavender lines mark the equator, tropics and polar circles.
