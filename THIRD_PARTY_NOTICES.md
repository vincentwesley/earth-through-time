## Data: Merdith et al. (2021) 1 Ga plate model (`public/frames/`)
Derived from the plate model files v1.2.4 accompanying: Merdith, A.S., Williams, S.E., Collins, A.S., Tetley, M.G., Mulder, J.A., Blades, M.L., Young, A., Armistead, S.E., Cannon, J., Zahirovic, S., Müller, R.D. (2021). Extending full-plate tectonic models into deep time: Linking the Neoproterozoic and the Phanerozoic. Earth-Science Reviews 214, 103477. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.earscirev.2020.103477
- Source: https://www.earthbyte.org/webdav/ftp/Data_Collections/Merdith_etal_2021_ESR/SM2-Merdith_et_al_1_Ga_reconstruction_v1.2.4.zip (Zenodo: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13635864)
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (as stated on the Zenodo record). EarthByte's general data pages state CC BY 3.0 Unported; both require only attribution, which is given here and in the README.
- Used files: rotation model, `shapes_continents.gpml`, `shapes_coastlines_Merdith_et_al_v2.gpmlz`, `shapes_static_polygons_Merdith_et_al.gpml` (only to find Singapore's plate). The coastline and continent shapes ship with the model itself; no GSHHG or other separately licensed layer is used.
- Changes: polygons simplified (~16 km tolerance) and triangulated; finite rotations sampled every 1 Myr from 750 Ma to present and stored as quantised quaternions. Generated by `pipeline/build_frames.py`.
